About this data

The first 18 variants (Kia EV5 and EV6, Skoda Enyaq, BYD Sealion 7, Toyota bZ4X, Subaru Trailseeker and Volkswagen ID.4) come from research compiled on 21 September 2026, mostly from ACT configurator screenshots and dealer quotes. The remaining variants were added on 24 October 2026 from the public sources listed below.

Driving and comfort findings (seating position, visibility, instrument display and head-up display, adaptive cruise, infotainment, screen format, physical controls, CarPlay and audio) were added on 24 October 2026. Head-up display is recorded per grade where the review names the grade. They come from the earlier research, from Australian reviews read on that date (CarExpert, The Beep, GoAuto, RACQ, Chasing Cars) and from own test drives. Each finding says whether several outlets, one outlet or an own test drive reported it. One outlet is a lead, not a settled view, so check it on a test drive. Findings are held per model and cover only the models listed with a result, which is a minority of the variants here. Reviews of an older model year may not match the current car. Cybersecurity is a brand-level finding from public investigations and reporting, and the absence of a report is not evidence that a brand is secure.

Prices are on different bases. Each variant states its basis: ACT, Sydney or NSW drive-away, drive-away with no state given, or before on-road costs. A price before on-road costs excludes registration, stamp duty and delivery, so it reads lower than a drive-away price for the same car. Use the drive-away filter to compare prices on one basis.

A match score only uses criteria that have data for that variant. A dash, or a dashed marker, means no figure was found after a second search on 24 October 2026, which is different from a failed match. Where sources disagree, the card says so and the figure is left blank. Relative preferences such as "prefer lower" compare each variant with the others in this list, not with the whole market.

Prices, offers and warranty terms change often, so confirm them with a dealer. Kia servicing figures are the seven-service prepaid price divided by seven, which assumes a service every year.

Sources: CarExpert, Carsales, EV Central, The Beep, Chasing Cars, GoAuto, The Driven, ANCAP, NRMA, Tynan, The EV Report, Kia prepaid service plans, and the Tesla Australia warranty page.
